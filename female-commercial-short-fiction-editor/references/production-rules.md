# 商业规则速查

## 开篇

Use: **具体恶行 + 不合理要求 + 女主即时损失 + 反击承诺 / 异常信息**.

Within 300 characters, include one villain action, one reader anger point, and one question that promises a reversal.

## 痛点优先级

Prefer concrete female-audience pain points: care-work exploitation, mother-in-law control, absent husband, favoritism, family exclusion, workplace gender discipline, reproductive coercion, reputation attacks, cyberbullying, identity theft, and property deprivation.

Materialize pain through money, objects, time, named responsibilities, chat logs, recordings, bills, doors, beds, custody, or documents.

## Escalation and payoff

- 0–1,000: anger + promise of counterattack.
- 1,000–3,000: first closed-loop payoff.
- 3,000–6,000: escalation and information advantage.
- 6,000–9,000: core reversal and public/legal evidence.
- 9,000–10,000: settlement and new life.

Use mirror retaliation, rule return, information asymmetry, evidence reversal, public exposure, identity reversal, property settlement, and life restart. Match punishment to wrongdoing.

## Guardrails

- Do not use vague villains or vague victories.
- Do not rely on coincidence where evidence, planning, or a resource can do the work.
- Do not stack several unrelated catastrophes into one climax.
- Do not make an apology erase concrete harm.
- End with at least two visible heroine gains: boundary, money, reputation, safety, work, housing, custody, or a supportive relationship.
